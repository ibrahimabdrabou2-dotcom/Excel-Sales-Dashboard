# Excel Sales Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer activity, and regional trends to support data-driven business decisions.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to provide a consolidated view of business performance across product categories, sub-categories, customers, cities, states, and years.

The dashboard helps users monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), compare sales across different segments, and explore historical sales and quantity trends through interactive filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales, net sales, profit, discounts, returns, and cost of goods sold.
- Compare sales performance across product categories and sub-categories.
- Identify leading cities and states by sales.
- Analyze sales across different regions and sales representatives.
- Track sales and quantity trends over time.
- Enable interactive exploration of the data using slicers and filters.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Product Category
Compares sales performance across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

### 2. Sales by Region and Sales Representative
Explores sales performance across regions and individual sales representatives.

### 3. Top 15 Cities by Sales
Ranks the leading cities based on sales volume to highlight major sales markets.

### 4. Top 15 States by Sales
Compares state-level sales performance and identifies the highest-performing markets.

### 5. Top 5 Sub-Categories
Visualizes the distribution of sales across selected product sub-categories.

### 6. Sales by State and Category
Compares product category performance across leading states.

### 7. Sales and Quantity Trends
Tracks yearly sales and quantity trends from 2014 to 2017.

### 8. Interactive Filtering
Uses slicers to filter the dashboard by person, city, and order date, allowing users to explore different segments of the business.
